Scenario 19: Ignoring CC-BY-NC Licenses
A digital agency uses a "Non-Commercial" Creative Commons photo for a massive corporate ad campaign.
Decision A (Ethical): Buy a commercial stock photo instead.
Immediate: The agency spends a small amount of money.
Explanation: Ethical sourcing requires paying fair market value for creative assets used to generate profit.
Ripple: The campaign proceeds without legal hitches.
Explanation: Clearing proper commercial rights ensures the marketing campaign can run unhindered.
Long-Term: The agency maintains a stellar reputation for IP compliance.
Explanation: Respecting licensing restrictions builds trust with corporate clients who rely on the agency to keep them legally safe.
Decision B (Unethical): Use the CC-BY-NC photo anyway, assuming the creator won't notice.
Immediate: The agency saves money and launches the campaign.
Explanation: Ignoring non-commercial clauses is a common form of corporate theft from independent creators.
Ripple: The creator's fans notice and cause a social media uproar.
Explanation: The internet is highly protective of independent artists, leading to rapid public backlash against the agency.
Long-Term: The client drops the agency due to the bad PR, and the creator sues for commercial damages.
Explanation: Flagrant violation of Creative Commons licenses destroys client trust and results in heavy financial penalties.
Decision C (Mixed): Contact the creator and offer a tiny one-time fee to waive the NC clause.
Immediate: The creator accepts, needing the money.
Explanation: The agency exploits the artist's financial desperation to buy commercial rights far below market value.
Ripple: The creator later realizes the massive scale of the campaign and feels cheated.
Explanation: While technically a legal contract, the bad-faith negotiation damages relationships within the creative community.
Long-Term: The agency is technically legally safe but suffers reputational damage in the creative community.
Explanation: The agency avoids a lawsuit but becomes known as an exploitative entity among digital artists.
